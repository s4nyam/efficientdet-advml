# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in
a response appropriate to the circumstances. The maintainer is obligated to
maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.
